Youth DJ & Hip Hop Program

Culture taught through practice, history and responsibility.

SOURFACEMUSIC teaches DJ culture and hip hop history to young people in the Bronx, offering instruction in DJ fundamentals, beat matching, equipment setup, sampling, performance technique and cultural literacy. The program emphasizes confidence, discipline, teamwork, cultural authenticity and creative responsibility.
